8037|599|114|12|Bold|What I learned
8067|599|672|25|Bold|Design the failure states first
8115|599|672|16|Regular|On a field app, the happy path is the easy part. The decisions that earned staff trust were the unhappy ones: what a queued payment looks like, who owns a rejected one, and how a supervisor sees the same problem the officer does. Defining the sync states and the seven failure screens before polishing the main flows gave engineering a contract to build against, and gave the bank confidence that offline work wouldn't turn into missing money. I'd bring that order — states, then failures, then screens — to any product where trust is the feature.
