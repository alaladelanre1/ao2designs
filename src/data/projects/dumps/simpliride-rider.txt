11731|605|115|12|Bold|What I Learned
11757|605|436|25|Bold|Trust is a rider feature, not a driver one
11819|605|661|16|Regular|Designing the rider side after the driver side made one thing obvious: a subscription model that makes driver economics fairer doesn't mean anything to a rider unless the app also gives them something they didn't have before — real say over price, and safety tools they don't have to dig for. The Offer Fare mechanic turned out to be less about undercutting the standard fare and more about giving riders a sense of urgency in a transaction that usually feels like it's happening to them. Designing both sides of the same marketplace also made the trade-offs sharper — every feature that builds rider trust (verification, live tracking, SOS) has a direct mirror on the driver side, and getting the two to feel like one coherent system, not two separate apps, was the real work.
